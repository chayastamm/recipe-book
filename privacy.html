Recipe Book

Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

Recipe Book is a free website for saving your recipes and downloading them as a PDF. This page explains what it accesses, where your data is stored, and how to remove it.

No servers, no tracking

Recipe Book runs entirely in your web browser. It has no server or database of its own, and it doesn't use cookies, analytics, or advertising. Your recipes are never sent to the site's owner.

What Recipe Book accesses in your Google account

When you sign in with Google, you're asked to allow:

  • Your name and email address. These are shown at the top of the page and saved with each recipe you add (as "Added by").
  • Google Drive files that Recipe Book creates (the drive.file permission). Recipe Book can only see and change files it made itself. It can't see any of your other Drive files.

Where your data is stored

Everything is saved in your own Google Drive, in a folder called Recipe Book:

  • recipes.json holds your recipes: titles, categories, ingredients, instructions, notes, and the email address of whoever added each one.
  • Photos you add to recipes are saved as image files in the same folder.

Your sign-in token is kept in your browser's session storage. It's cleared when you sign out or close the tab, and it expires after about an hour.

Sharing

Recipe Book doesn't sell, share, or transfer your data to anyone. Your recipes are only visible to people you choose to share your Drive folder or PDF with.

Recipe Book's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services the site uses

  • Google handles sign-in and stores your files. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) supplies the PDF-making code.

Like any website host, these services may log basic technical details such as your IP address when your browser loads the page.

Removing your data

  • To delete your recipes and photos, delete the Recipe Book folder in your Google Drive.
  • To stop Recipe Book from accessing your account, remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
